Overview

ABOUT THE DREAMER

Welcome... the dreamer whose subconscious I reside in is known as Luc.

I am Dius, a robot assistant that handles Luc's subconscious.

I handle making sure each dream is released so that a leak doesn't happen, as that would be rather catastrophic and would lead to not so pleasant dreams flowing about.

Luc is 23 years old, an Aries and INFJ. This is the method of releasing the overflowing subconscious so it does not become overwhelming.

The goal is to share the dreams so that others may partake if they wish.

ABOUT THE DREAMLAND

Each dream exists behind a door somewhere within Luc's subconscious.

The residents are available to communicate with, but are unaware that they exist within a dream.

From time to time, if I am unable to release a dream in time, one may become corrupted and escape on its own.

That occasionally leads to themes unusual for Luc's subconscious.

NOTE FROM DIUS

If you wish to inspire a dream, you are more than welcome.
